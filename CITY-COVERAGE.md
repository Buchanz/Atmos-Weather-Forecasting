# City coverage

243 photographed cities are currently selectable, across 91 countries and territories. All selectable cities have a locally bundled photograph.

## Worldwide expansion target

The target dataset contains 4377 national capitals, first-level regional capitals (states, provinces and territories), and cities with populations of at least 500,000, across 245 countries and territories. This is a **target, not a claim of complete photo coverage**. 4140 target places still need an approved photograph. See [the remaining target list](scripts/data/missing-photos.json).

Location data: [GeoNames cities1000](https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/), snapshot 2026-09-22, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Population estimates and administrative classifications vary by source and date.
